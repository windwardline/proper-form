Grooming Fundamentals

The Seamless Beard Fade

A sharp line at the jaw makes a face look fuller. Fading the beard into the neck creates a strong, masculine jawline. Here is how to execute it at home.

  1. 1 The Base: Trim your entire beard to your desired length using a single guard (e.g., #3 or #4).
  2. 2 The Hard Line: Establish your neckline exactly one inch above your Adam's apple. Shave everything below this line bald.
  3. 3 The Blend: Put a #1 guard on. Open the lever. Flick the clippers upward for about half an inch above your hard line to blur the transition.
