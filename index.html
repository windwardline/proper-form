Grooming: The Seamless Beard Fade

A sharp presentation stops at the neckline if your grooming is neglected. Fading your beard into your neckline creates a sculpted, intentional jawline.

  1. 1 The Base: Start with your longest guard (e.g., #3 or #4) and trim the entire beard to an even length to remove flyaways.
  2. 2 The Hard Line: Take the guard off entirely (clipper closed). Look straight in the mirror. Trim a hard line exactly one inch above your Adam's apple. Shave everything below this line bald.
  3. 3 The Blend: Put a #1 guard on. Open the lever. Flick the clippers upward for about half an inch above your hard line to blur the transition.
