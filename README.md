Project Scope

Project Name: Proper Form: The Modern Gentleman's Guide

Project Framing: Proper Form is a trustworthy, judgment-free guide for men who were never taught the basics of presentation but need to show up with confidence.

One-Sentence Description: An educational, high-end web platform that demystifies seasonal style, proper fit, and grooming fundamentals to help men elevate their personal presentation with confidence.

MVP Personas & User Stories

Eli (The First-Step Professional): A college student or recent graduate preparing for interviews, internships, networking events, or a first office role. Eli needs a reliable resource that explains style, fit, and grooming basics without assuming prior knowledge.User story: As a young professional, I want clear explanations of basic menswear and grooming rules, so that I can feel prepared and confident in professional settings.

Raymond (The Reentry Candidate): A recently released man preparing for job interviews, family events, or community settings after years of wearing institutional clothing. Raymond needs practical, respectful guidance on how to present himself as mature, stable, and rehabilitated.User story: As someone reentering society, I want plain-language guidance on fit, grooming, and mature presentation, so that I can show up with dignity and confidence.

Maurice (The Summer Wedding Guest): A man attending a summer wedding who wants to dress appropriately without overheating or looking too casual. Maurice needs clear seasonal advice on fabrics, loafers, fit, and grooming.User story: As a summer wedding guest, I want simple guidance on summer suiting, fit rules, and grooming, so that I can look polished and appropriate for the event.

Features:

Must Have (I will not present without these):
Seasonal Style Section: Guidance on Summer suiting (linen/cotton blends, loafer etiquette).
The Rules & Fit Section: Clear, actionable advice on common faux pas (e.g., the bottom button rule, shoulder fit).
Grooming Fundamentals: Step-by-step guidance on modern grooming, specifically how to execute a seamless beard fade at home.
Responsive Navigation: A mobile-friendly hamburger menu that toggles smoothly.

Nice to Have (Only if must-haves are done):
Dark mode toggle.
Interactive "Style Quiz" to recommend specific cuts based on body type.

Pages/Sections (Single Page Architecture):

Header: Logo/Title and Navigation links.
Hero: High-impact typographic intro setting the premium tone, welcoming our core personas.
Summer Sartorial: Cards/grid explaining summer fabrics and rules.
The Fit: Visual/textual breakdown of suit rules.
Grooming: Guide on beard maintenance.
Footer: Copyright and social links.

Tech:
HTML, CSS (Tailwind CSS via CDN), Vanilla JavaScript.Deployed on GitHub/Vercel.
